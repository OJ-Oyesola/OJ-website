Terms & Conditions

Last updated: March 2026

1. Booking & Retainer

A non-refundable retainer fee (60% of the total invoice) and a digitally signed contract are strictly required to secure and reserve your session date and time. Dates are booked on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be held without a retainer.

2. Payment Terms

The remaining balance of your selected photography collection is due in full prior to the session date (for portraits) or no later than 14 days prior to the event date (for weddings). Final galleries will not be processed or delivered until the balance is paid in full.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellations

In the event that the Client reschedules the session, the retainer fee may be transferred to a new date, provided the Photographer is available and notice is given at least 48 hours in advance for portraits, and 30 days in advance for weddings. For cancellations, the retainer fee is non-refundable, as it serves as liquidated damages for the Photographer turning away other potential bookings for that reserved date.

4. Copyright & Image Usage

The Photographer (OJ_Oyesola) retains the absolute copyright to all images produced. The Client is granted a personal use license, allowing them to download, print, and share the images online for personal, non-commercial purposes. Clients may not alter, edit, or apply filters to the final delivered images, as this misrepresents the Photographer's artistry and brand.

5. Delivery & Turnaround Time

Standard turnaround time for portrait sessions is typically 1–2 weeks, while weddings and large events require 2–4 weeks for comprehensive editing and processing. Faster turnaround, unless agreed upon, attracts a 25% fastracking fee. All images are delivered via a private, high-resolution online gallery. The gallery will remain active for 12 months from the date of delivery.

6. Artistic Discretion

The Client acknowledges that they have reviewed the Photographer's portfolio and are booking based on the Photographer's established aesthetic, editing style, and artistic vision. The Photographer retains full creative control over the editing process, and no unedited, RAW files will be provided under any circumstances.
