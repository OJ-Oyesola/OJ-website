Last updated: September 2026

1. Booking & Retainer

A non-refundable retainer fee of sixty percent (60%) of the total invoice, together with a digitally signed contract, is required to secure and reserve your session date and time. Dates are booked on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be held without payment of the retainer.

2. Payment Terms

The remaining balance is due in full prior to the session date (for portraits) or no later than fourteen (14) days before the event date (for weddings and events). Final galleries will not be processed or delivered until the balance is paid in full.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellations

The Client may transfer the retainer to a new date, provided the Photographer is available and written notice is given at least 48 hours in advance for portrait sessions, or 30 days in advance for weddings and events. In the event of cancellation, the retainer fee is non-refundable, as it compensates the Photographer for turning away other potential bookings for that reserved date.

4. Copyright & Image Usage

The Photographer retains absolute copyright to all images produced. The Client is granted a personal-use license to download, print, and share the final delivered images online for personal, non-commercial purposes. The Client shall not alter, edit, crop, or apply filters to the final delivered images, as this misrepresents the Photographer's artistry and brand.

5. Delivery & Turnaround

Standard turnaround time is 1–2 weeks for portrait sessions and 2–4 weeks for weddings and events. Faster delivery, unless previously agreed upon, attracts a 25% rush fee. All images are delivered via an access-coded, high-resolution online gallery, which will remain active for twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

6. Artistic Discretion

The Client acknowledges they have reviewed the Photographer's portfolio and are booking based on the Photographer's established aesthetic, editing style, and artistic vision. The Photographer retains full creative control over the editing process. No unedited or RAW files will be provided under any circumstances.
