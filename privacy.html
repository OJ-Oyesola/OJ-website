Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire about my photography services, book a session, or communicate with me, I may ask for certain personal information, including but not limited to your name, email address, phone number, event locations, and details relevant to your session.

2. How We Use Your Information

I strictly use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes.
  • Coordinate and plan your photography sessions or events.
  • Deliver your final image galleries seamlessly.
  • Send important administrative or billing correspondence.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Your privacy is deeply respected. I do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only where strictly necessary to deliver your images and fulfil my professional services to you — for example, the secure client-gallery system on this website, or trusted third-party gallery-hosting platforms (such as Pic-Time or Pixieset) when used for your delivery.

4. Image Usage and Privacy

As the creator, OJ_Oyesola retains the copyright to all images. However, I respect the privacy of my clients. While I may kindly request to use select images from your session for my portfolio, website, or social media, I will never do so if you explicitly request a fully private session prior to booking.

5. Security

I take reasonable precautions to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All client galleries are delivered via secure, access-coded links, and private galleries can be further restricted at your request.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the handling of your data, please contact me directly at oj.oyesola@gmail.com.
